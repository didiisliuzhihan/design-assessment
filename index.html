The design hypothesis

Less searching.
A more explicit human decision.

Interview excerpts suggest that managers sometimes need to check attachments, earlier messages, and pricing before approving a special quote. Bringing relevant sources into the review may reduce retrieval effort.

What the evidence does not prove

One logged approval spans 145 minutes. That interval includes waiting and availability; it does not establish 145 minutes of active checking.

Evidence: B2–B4 and L12. This proposal needs validation with people who perform the work.

Proposed workflow

01 / COORDINATORSubmit the exception

Link the quote and special customer requests.

02 / AI ASSISTANCEOrganize the evidence

Surface available sources and explicit gaps.

03 / MANAGERInspect and decide

Compare originals, flag concerns, approve or return.

04 / COORDINATORContinue the work

Revise or send the approved quote. Booking stays separate.
